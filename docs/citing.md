# Citing InstanSeg

If you use InstanSeg in your research, please cite the paper that matches your use.

**Nucleus segmentation in brightfield histology images:**

> Goldsborough, T. et al. (2024) "InstanSeg: an embedding-based instance segmentation algorithm
> optimized for accurate, efficient and portable cell segmentation". *arXiv*.
> <https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2408.15954>

**Nucleus and/or cell segmentation in fluorescence images:**

> Goldsborough, T. et al. (2024) "A novel channel invariant architecture for the segmentation of
> cells and nuclei in multiplexed images using InstanSeg". *bioRxiv*, p. 2024.09.04.611150.
> <https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.09.04.611150>
